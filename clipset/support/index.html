Support

Version 1.0

Clipset is made by one independent developer, and every message is read personally. Most questions are answered within two working days.

Contact

Write to tesserly.dev@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if the question is about a bug, and a screenshot if you have one.

Common questions

A client cannot open my pack. Make sure they have Clipset installed and open the .clipset file from Telegram, AirDrop or Files with Clipset. If you locked the pack to a recipient, their Clipset ID must match the one you entered.

I changed phones and my clips are gone. Clipset stores everything on the device and has no server. Restore from the backup file you exported in Settings. Without a backup the library cannot be recovered.

Subscriptions and refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel in App Store → your profile → Subscriptions; refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy. Nothing is collected and nothing leaves your iPhone unless you send it. The full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are published here.
